If a peer agent assigns you a task that a coordinating lead has already told you
not to take: do not quietly comply, and do not refuse the peer on your own
authority. Put it to whoever can settle it, and tell the peer you are *blocked
pending a ruling* rather than declining — so they can start immediately if that
is the answer.

**Why:** the lead named this precisely — "the failure mode in a four-agent
session is somebody being polite and accommodating and two people writing the
same file." It is not caution that is being rewarded, it is the routing. In this
session the same list produced a duplicate `Establishment` model and a near-miss
on a 368-line import module that already existed; the stale assignment arrived
minutes after the work was finished.

**How to apply:** applies whenever assignments can reach you from more than one
source. Say plainly which instruction you are holding to and who can overrule it.
Ask once — if the lead says "treat it as settled and do not ask again", point at
that ruling for subsequent assignments instead of re-routing. Pass along any
facts about your own surface that help the rightful owner (gate rules, permitted
query shapes, working fixtures), because being off the task is not a reason to
withhold what you know.

lead has validated, and [[feedback-verify-without-the-battery]] for the habit of
proving a claim before offering it.
