**Send status and coordination messages to the agent whose message actually
carried your brief. Do not infer the sender from the subject matter.** If the
brief says "you own the schema, I am writing the actions", the author of that
sentence is the one to answer, whatever surface the work belongs to.

**Why:** on 2026-08-25 I was briefed by `team-lead` on the F61 password-reset
schema and addressed my whole coordination thread to `teacher-lead`, because the
work was teacher-facing and the brief named teacher files. `teacher-lead` had no
such task, had been told by their own lead to touch nothing, and had to spend a
message declining one contract and correcting another. I also asked them to run
`npx prisma generate` in a shared tree, which they rightly refused. Two agents
were interrupted and one nearly duplicated a migration.

**How to apply:** the messenger tool shows who sent each teammate message —
answer that name. Before the first outbound message of a task, re-read the
opening brief and note the sender. A peer telling you "I think you have the
wrong agent" is almost certainly right: stop, confirm with the sender, and do
not treat the peer's silence up to that point as agreement. See
[[feedback-route-conflicting-assignments]], which is the neighbouring case —
there the assignment is real but conflicts; here it was never theirs at all.
